## What was built
| # | Change | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **New `POST /study-profile`.** The app sends `{"text": "<what was said after Lumen>", "profile"?: {grade, course, subject, exam_date}}` and gets back `{"profile": {"grade", "course", "subject", "exam_date", "no_exam"}, "terms": [...]}`. `grade` uses the app's `StudyProfile.Grade` values (`y6`…`y9`, `gy1`…`gy3`, `university`, `other`). The exam date is an absolute ISO date from the server's Swedish date. There are **30–50 central terms** for the grade and subject, following Skolverket's central content, for Scribe keyterms and `prep_terms`. `context.prep_terms` now takes up to **50** terms (it was 30). Haiku; nothing stored. | verified live |
| 2 | **Subject first:** with a Provläge profile, a misheard, split or very short word is first read as a term of the profile's subject and of `prep_terms`, before any general meaning. The rule travels with the profile in the user message, so the cached prompt is unchanged. The prompt example is my own ("mito" in Biologi); the owner's "syntes" is kept for the eval. | verified live |
| 3 | **Texts and quotes:** a request to sing, recite or quote, or for the words of a song or poem, is detected in code (`needsText`). It gets its own rules and search: text archives such as runeberg.org, litteraturbanken.se, wikisource, gutenberg.org, isof.se and svenskakyrkan.se, plus encyclopedias. It runs on the **stronger model (Sonnet 5.5, like Rizz)**, because Haiku got the words of Helan går wrong and answered Strindberg in English. **Traditional and public-domain texts are given in full**; "sjung" means say it with the rhythm; a traditional song is never refused. Protected works get at most one or two quoted sentences, with title and author. **Modern song lyrics are never given**: the answer is title, artist and what the song is about. A quote only when the search confirms it; otherwise the work and a paraphrase. | verified live |
| 4 | **New `POST /exam`**, four actions:<br>• `parse` — at most 10 page images (jpeg/png/webp, ≤ 3 MB each) per call, read once by Haiku vision. It returns the tasks (number, text, points per E/C/A, correct answer, scoring rules) and the grade limits. The app sends the exam and the instructions in batches and merges by task number.<br>• `hint` — level 1, 2 or 3, never the final answer.<br>• `grade` — compares an answer with the scoring rules. **The final number is checked in code** (digits or Swedish number words, 0.5 % tolerance; algebraic answers are not checked as numbers), and the points are clamped in code to what the task gives.<br>• `summary` — **code only, no model**: totals per level, the grade reached against the limits, and what is missing to the next grade, as a spoken sentence.<br>**No exam is stored on the server**: the app keeps the parsed result and sends the task with each call; `answer_usage` only gets metadata. | verified live with my own 3-page test exam |
| — | The LLM provider now accepts images (`LLMRequest.images`, 120 s timeout). | tested |

## Cost
- **Per uploaded exam:** about **0.00027 USD per page** (Haiku vision: 3 pages = 4 956 input + 619 output tokens =
  0.0008 USD). A typical national exam with instructions, about 20–30 pages, costs about **0.006–0.01 USD** to read
  once.
- Per hint or grading: about 0.0001–0.0002 USD. A summary is free (no model).
- Per text or quote request: about **0.01 USD** (Sonnet and up to 2 searches at 0.01 USD each).
- Per `/study-profile` call: about 0.0004 USD.
- All D84 live tests: about **0.10 USD**. Most of it is the text evals on Sonnet with search, about 0.085.

## Known limits and risks
- **Texts:** Sonnet still sometimes writes a wrong line in a traditional song when it does not search. A quote can be
  attributed wrongly, though rarer after the tightened rule. Text requests are slower (3–6 s to the first word).
- The `källa:` line of a text answer is spoken in some runs ("Källa: Runeberg. runeberg.org"); the tap expects one
  domain on its own line.
- The exam test used a clean, typed synthetic exam. A real national exam with figures, tables and handwriting-like
  scans is **not tested**. Large pages cost more tokens; the app should scale pages to about 1 600 px.
- The exam's level-3 hint is close to the answer.
- `exam_date` follows the model's reading of relative days ("nästa fredag" can be ambiguous).
- The study profile's term list is the model's idea of Skolverket's central content, not checked against the
  curriculum.
