## What was built
| # | Change | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The profile becomes `<profile>`** in Provläge (`mode: "tutor"`, `persona: "tutor"`, `persona_setup`). It sits in the user message after the cached system prompt, together with `<profile_rules>`. **The cached Provläge system prompt is exactly the same as before D83**, so a call without a profile is unchanged. Other modes never get the profile. | verified in tests |
| 2 | **Level:** every answer is at the level Skolverket's knowledge requirements ask for grade A in exactly that grade and course. It is explained so the student can say it in their own words on the test: the key point first, then why or how, with the subject's terms. Åk 6–9 stays at the grundskola level (no named steps, molecules or mechanisms from gymnasiet courses); Gymnasiet and Högskola go into the mechanisms. | verified live (Åk 7 vs Gy 3) |
| 3 | **Check questions:** "inga" = never one; this is also enforced in code, because the closing question is cut, as in text mode. "ja–nej" = answerable with one word or a nod (Angela's nod detection); "korta" = a word or a short phrase; "svåra" = needs reasoning at A level. | prompt + code (inga) |
| 4 | **Explanation length:** "korta" means at most three sentences and about 50 words, "mellanlånga" four to six sentences, "utförliga" seven to ten. | partly: see limits |
| 5 | **Exam date:** today, tomorrow or within a few days gives priority to what most likely comes on the test (core concepts, typical exam questions, common mistakes). | prompt only |
| 6 | **Live eval:** a new category `profil`. `profil-01` asks "förklara fotosyntesen" for Åk 7 and must not mention Calvin, NADPH, tylakoid or rubisco. `profil-02` asks the same for Gymnasiet 3 (Biologi 2) and must mention ljusreaktion/Calvin/ATP/NADPH/tylakoid. `profil-03` (Åk 8 kemi, no check questions, short explanations) has a word limit and no question. | done |

## Known limits and risks
- "korta" answers are often three long sentences (70–80 words). A hard cap in code would cut mid-sentence; if it is
  too long on the device, I add a cap of three sentences in code.
- The level follows the prompt and Skolverket as Haiku knows it; there is no check against the curriculum.
- The nod for yes–no questions is the app's work (D83 (2)).
