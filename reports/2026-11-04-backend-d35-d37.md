## Done

- `mode: "tutor"` (D36): headerless, always study/pedagogical, whole-session `recent_qa`, optional check question.
- D37: `goals` (<=1 500) and `commands` (<=10) validated, sanitised and tagged; no-filler rules; check questions only ja/nej-or-one-word.
- General rules instead of special cases (most likely meaning, bare term = explain, continuity incl. reply to a confirmation, confirmations pruned from memory). Hold-out rule enforced by a test (no pi/decimals/fission/owner examples); my examples: enzymes, cells, electric cars.
- Body cap 64 KB (worst-case legal request is ~49 KB with åäö), `meta.answer_type`, confidence threshold 0.45. README + D40 + test sets in `testdata/scripts/` (15 study fragments, 10-turn conversation).

## Findings / limits
- First run showed openings that commented on or repeated the fragment ("Varför är en fråga som kan syfta på flera saker …", "Och pH fungerar …") and silly check questions; tightened prompt (v10): openings clean in the re-run; check questions now fewer (4/10) but 1 of 4 still vague ("Vilket av dessa två namn …").
- `mitos` without context is answered as mitochondria; with `goals` (cell biology) it is mitosis. Haiku, not the prompt; goals are the fix.
- Study answers cost more TTS credits (~900 chars each); app should stream sentence by sentence (already) and the owner may want a per-day char cap review.
- Not measured: on-device behaviour, real TTS length, `auto` mode with the new prompt (unit/e2e only).
