## What was built
| # | Change | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Socialt/Rizz, speaker-labelled transcript.** In `social.transcript` each line's `speaker` may now be `"user"`/`"other"` as before, or the app's label (`"Du"` = the user, `"Talare 1"`, `"Talare 2"` …); `"other"` lines can also carry `label`. `social.speaker` names who said the fragment. The model sees "Jag:", "Talare 1:", "Talare 2:" and is told they are different people: answer the one who spoke last, about what that person said. **A suggestion that says a label aloud ("Talare ett") is never shown** (code filter). Without labels, nothing changes ("Den andra"). | verified live |
| 2 | **Intervjuläge with CV:** `persona_setup` limit raised from 1 500 to **2 000** characters. The prompt says "Jobbannons: … CV: …": the ad is the job and the CV is the user's own background (education, jobs, skills, dates), used as the user's facts and never added to. The D77/D79 rules (no invented facts) still apply. Angela's `InterviewProfile.setup` already sends this format (≤ 1 500 characters today; it can go to 2 000). | verified live: with and without CV |
| 3 | **New `POST /functions/v1/study-cards`** (user JWT). Request `{"summary": "<Provläge summary>", "profile"?: "<Provläge profile>", "cards"?: 3–12}`. Reply `{"cards": [{"q","a"}], "test": [{"q","a"}] ×5, "usage"}`. Haiku with a cached prompt; content only from the summary, at the level of the grade. Nothing is stored or logged (only a metadata row in `answer_usage`, endpoint `study-cards`). Limits: 4/min and 40/day per user, 600 per network and day, 3 000/day global. | verified live |
| 4 | **Interrupted answer not repeated:** a `context.recent_qa` item may carry `interrupted: true`, with `a` = what had been said when the app stopped it. The next request gets an `<interrupted>` note in the user message: never repeat that part, continue from where it stopped if asked, otherwise answer the fragment. The cached prompt is unchanged. **Angela:** send `interrupted: true` and the said text when the user stops an answer. | verified live |

## Known limits and risks
- Speaker labels are only as good as the app's voice tracking; a wrong label makes the line answer the wrong person.
- The CV is used as given; the model can still embellish a period ("sedan 2024" for 2024–2025).
- Study cards come from the summary only; a short summary gives few test questions.
- Rizz suggestion quality is not tuned here (waiting for Aron's examples).
