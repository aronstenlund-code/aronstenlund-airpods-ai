## Takeaways for Lumen
- Nobody in the library advertises **"answers in your AirPods in real time"** — the closest are recorders/note-takers (after-the-fact summaries). Positioning gap exists.
- Winning formats: short **UGC creator videos** (15–35 s, "this is the secret", WFH/student life), **App Store install** CTA, **testimonial stars**, discount codes via creators.
- Plaud proves willingness to pay for hardware-free alternatives? No — Plaud *is* hardware; Lumen's edge: no extra device, uses AirPods you already own.
- Angles to test later: "AirPods you already have → AI assistant", meetings/lectures notes + instant answers, discreet low-voice use on the go.
