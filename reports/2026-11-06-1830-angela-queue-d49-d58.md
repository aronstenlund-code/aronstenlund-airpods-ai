## Bugs found on the way
- **`goals` and `commands` never reached the server.** The app sent them inside `context`, the backend reads them at the top level, so D37 had no effect. Fixed. **verified in CI** against the backend's `validate.ts`.
- **A spoken report would have been lost.** `/answer` sends `meta` with `is_question:false` before `done.report`, and the engine stopped at `meta`. It now reads on to `done`.
- **The transcript outlived the session.** The consent text promised otherwise. It is now cleared when listening stops.
- **Spoken words went to the unified log as public text** (`SessionController.log`, TTS). Now private or left out.

## Decisions (D61)
- Own lines from Rizzläge and Intervjuläge reach Historik only from the **AirPods** microphone. On the iPhone mic the level tag cannot tell the two people apart, and D56 forbids saving the other person's words. With the default iPhone mic, Rizz saves only suggestions and a local summary.
- An unknown speaker counts as someone else: auto mode and "ask about the last sentence" save no question.
- Intervjuläge alternates question/answer strictly. There is no speaker detection; the checklist asks whether it drifts.
- Scribe uses `commit_strategy=vad` for continuous listening (the web test commits by hand) and commits on hold-to-ask release. **Untested assumption:** the parameter value is from memory.
- A Control or Siri phrase that starts listening opens the app first (D58 replaces "a Control never starts from idle" in D34). `supportedModes` replaces `openAppWhenRun`.

## Concerns
- **Discreet indicator: not built, decision needed.** The request was a small dot or thin frame without text, and no text in the app mentioning the indicator. A visible indicator is a documented guardrail (CLAUDE.md, D8, D21, D42, D43), and it is also what the App Store condition in R6 rests on. CLAUDE.md says to flag concealment instead of building it. Removing the Stop/Resume label and the sentence in the consent text would mainly make it harder to see that the app listens, in modes where other people are being heard. The bar and the consent sentence are unchanged. A slimmer bar that keeps the word "Lyssnar" and the Stop button is possible if the goal is less screen space.
- **Audio from other people now goes to ElevenLabs by default.** D44 overrides the old promise that audio never leaves the phone. Consent is bumped to version 4 and the text says so, but `enable_logging=false` is **not applied on the current plan** (Kevin, D50), so ElevenLabs may retain it. In Rizzläge those voices belong to people who did not sign anything. Worth a look before TestFlight.
- **Rizzläge outside a friend test.** The start screen asks the user to confirm that the other person knows. Nothing enforces it. D43 widens the use to everyday conversations, and that consent rests on the user alone.
- **Cost:** Scribe streams for the whole listening session, not only while holding the button: $0.39/hour (D44). Seconds are reported to `/stt-token`.
