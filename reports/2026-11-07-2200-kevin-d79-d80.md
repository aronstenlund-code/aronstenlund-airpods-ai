## D80: what was asked and built
| # | Change | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **New endpoint `POST /functions/v1/memory`** (`functions/memory/`). It runs Haiku 5.5 with a cached system prompt, outside the answer path. It takes the app's body exactly as Angela's `MemoryExtraction.swift` sends it (`turn.question`, `turn.answer`, `mode`, `memories`, `today` …; `q`/`a` also work). It returns `{"add": [...], "update": [{"old","new"}], "remove": [...]}`: at most 5 items of at most 160 characters each, dates always absolute from the server's Swedish date. It keeps only important facts about the wearer: tests, dates, subjects, level, goals, wishes, rules. **Never sensitive categories:** the prompt forbids them and code drops them as well. A turn with no self-reference ("vad är fotosyntes") gets `skipped: no_self_reference` without a model call. **Nothing is stored**: the text is processed and dropped, and only a metadata row is written to `answer_usage` (endpoint `memory`, tokens, cost; tested that the text is not logged). Limits: 10/min and 300/day per user, 3 000 per network and day, 10 000/day global. | verified live |
| 2 | **Memories and rules after the static system prompt:** this was already the case. `memories`, `commands` and `safety` are in the user message, and only the system prompt is cached. Measured live, see below: first token is not slower with 20 memories. | verified live |
| 3 | **Sound-alike mishearing without context:** the REPAIR rule now also covers this case. When the fragment makes no sense as written but clearly sounds like **one** school subject term, the model answers that term, even with no context and even when the fragment is only those words ("få tes" = fotosyntes, the owner's example). If several terms fit or none clearly fits, Provläge and Auto reply `[[TYST]]`. The model must never say that it interpreted, or what the words sound like, and code drops "Det låter som <Term> …". There are 12 new live-eval cases in the category `felhörning` (151 cases in total). | verified live: 11/12 |
| 4 | **Preliminary answers (the app's D80 "svara direkt, gör om"):** an answer the client aborts before `done` **does not count against the user's own minute and day windows**. Its units are given back in the window where they were counted (`release_rate_limits`, never below 0). **The network, global and new-user windows stay counted**, so a script cannot get free answers by aborting. The usage row gets status `aborted`. Aborts were already fast: `cancel()` aborts the Anthropic request at once. | **verified live** (see below) |
| 5 | **Feedback (D57):** new column `status` (`new`/`processed`) and `processed_at`. `POST /feedback-admin {"id","status":"processed"}` (service role key) marks a row processed once the problem is fixed. The new hourly `pg_cron` job `purge-feedback` deletes processed rows and **every row older than 90 days**. The privacy text in `backend/README.md` is updated: memory is never stored on the server; feedback is deleted when processed or after at most 90 days; shared history is kept 12 months as before. | verified in the database |

## Known limits and risks
- **The new-user cap (20 answers in the first hour) can be felt in heavy first use.** It applies only during the first
  10 minutes after sign-in. Auto has a local gate with a 5-second cooldown, but a fast Provläge drill or a Socialt/Rizz
  conversation can make more than 20 calls in 10 minutes. The app shows a notice and falls back. Preliminary answers
  that the app aborts still count against this window (it is not released). If that is noticed, raise it with
  `NEW_USER_ANSWERS_PER_HOUR`, for example to 40; it does not need a deploy.
- A school on one IP shares 1 500 answers and 30 000 TTS characters a day. That is about 50 answers each for 30
  students; the TTS cap is the tighter one, about 1 000 characters each. Raise it per env before a school rollout.
- **Oscar's QA scripts** run many turns on one new user. They now hit the new-user cap after 20 answers, so spread the
  calls over more users or wait 10 minutes.
- `/memory`: whether "nästa tisdag" means the 10th or the 17th is ambiguous in Swedish, and the model chose the 17th.
  `update` and `remove` are not read by the app yet (it decodes only `add`), which is a small task for Angela. The app's
  own sensitive-word filter and mine differ a little; both drop.
- The mishearing rule follows the prompt, so a clearly misheard word can still be silent and a wrong reading can be
  answered (the "pytt i panna" case). The keyterms in the app (D80.6) are the main fix for "få tes".
- The aborted-answer refund gives back only the minute and day windows; if the window has turned over meanwhile,
  nothing is given back.
- The live sign-in limit (about 30 per hour per IP) limits how many eval runs can be made from one machine. I used
  `evalfew.py` (2 users) for the last run.
