Get started

Five short steps from the first screen to your first answer in your AirPods.

  1. Install and open EarMind

    The iPhone app is coming soon to the App Store. When it's out, install it and open it. Read the welcome screen and tap Continue. No account is needed.

  2. Connect your AirPods

    Put in your AirPods. They connect to your iPhone as usual. In EarMind, open Control with your AirPods to try the presses: once for the long answer, twice for the short one, three times to pause. No AirPods? Answers play on the phone's speaker.

  3. Ask your first question

    The next screen explains what happens to sound and text. Tap I understand, start EarMind, allow the microphone, and ask out loud, for example “What is photosynthesis?” The answer comes in your ear and on the screen. You always see “● Listening”, and one tap stops it. Want to study without the microphone? Tap Later, then New quiz.

  4. Set your activation words

    Under “Question you're not prepared for? Say [ … ] so our AI activates.” type one or more words of your own, for example Help me. When EarMind hears them while it listens, it answers the question that was just asked.

  5. Record it yourself – completely free

    On the first screen, tap Record it yourself. Write a question and an answer, tap Record and read them aloud, then tap Practice. No credits, and the sound stays on your phone.
