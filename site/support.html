Getting started

What is EarMind?
EarMind helps you study by listening in your AirPods. Snap your textbook and EarMind creates quiz questions with answers and reads them in your ear. Or type any text in Say in AirPods and hear it again and again.
How do I make my first quiz?
  1. Tap New quiz.
  2. Add material: Take photos, Choose photos, Add document (PDF, Word or text) or Type text.
  3. Tap Create questions. You can leave the app while it works.
  4. Check Is this right? and tap Looks right, or write a correction.
  5. Tap Start quiz.
I stopped a quiz. How do I go on?
Start shows the quiz you played last at the top, with Continue. When you tap End, you see how many questions you heard and can tap Practice those … again for the ones you asked for the answer to. It uses the same audio, so it costs nothing.
How does Say in AirPods work?
Type or paste a text and tap Play for 10 min. It repeats until the time is up, from 10 minutes up to an hour. Write how it should sound under How should it sound?, or pick a voice and preview it for free. Long text? Tap Compress for a shorter one with the same content.

AirPods

How do I hear the answers?
While a question plays or right after it: press once for the long answer, twice for the short answer and three times to pause. Press again to continue. The answers are only read when you press.
Do I need AirPods?
No. Without AirPods the app plays through the phone's speaker.
Older AirPods with a double tap on each earbud
Set the left earbud to Next Track (short answer) and the right to Play/Pause (long answer and pause) in iPhone Settings › Bluetooth › ⓘ next to your AirPods.
A press doesn't get through
Check that both AirPods are in your ears and connected. If another app is playing music, the presses go to that app: start EarMind again and they come back. You can try the presses first under Control with your AirPods.

Your activation words

What are activation words?
In More modes (beta) the app says: “Question you're not prepared for? Say [ … ] so our AI activates.” Type one or more words of your own in the field, or under Settings › Activation words. When EarMind hears them while it listens, it answers the question that was just asked.
When does it listen?
Only in More modes, after you have said yes and started it. You always see “● Listening” at the top, and you stop it with one tap. Start quiz and Say in AirPods never use the microphone.

Record it yourself – completely free

What is it?
Write your own questions and answers, record them in your own voice and practice them in your AirPods, just like a quiz.
Does it cost anything?
No. It uses no credits. Anything you haven't recorded is read by the phone's own voice.
Where does the sound go?
Nowhere. Your recordings stay on your phone and are never sent to us.

Credits and the launch offer

How do credits work?
1 credit is 1 character of new audio. You get 1,500 credits every month, and they refill on the 1st. Repeats, audio that already exists, quizzes others have shared, Compress, voice previews and Record it yourself are free.
What if I run out?
The app shows what something costs before anything is made, and nothing is taken if you don't have enough. Tap Top up for credit packs or the Weekly pass. Prices are shown in the app, and you pay with your Apple account. The weekly pass renews every week until you cancel in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
Is there a launch offer?
During launch week, everything is 65% off. The app and this website show it while the week lasts.

When our server can't answer

Reading aloud
If the voice can't read right now, the phone's own voice reads instead, and the app tells you. No credits are used for it.
Answers and grading
On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the phone's own model answers in More modes and grades your answers, and the app says so. No credits are used for it.
Compress and new quizzes
If the server can't shorten your text, the phone's model does it where it's available. If a new quiz can't be made right now, try again in a moment, or use Record it yourself.

Privacy

Is my material saved?
No. Photos, documents and text are sent once to make the questions and are then deleted.
Is any sound saved?
EarMind never saves what the microphone hears. In More modes the sound is sent to ElevenLabs to be turned into text, and EarMind keeps only the text. Your own recordings in Record it yourself stay on your phone.
What is saved?
Your quizzes, with questions, answers and audio, are saved with your account so they're there on a new phone. You don't need to sign up with an email.
Where are my questions and answers from More modes?
In Settings › History, on this phone only, for up to two years. You can search, play an answer again and delete it all.
Can I delete everything?
Yes. In the app: Settings › Account › Delete account deletes the account and everything on our server.

Quick question? In the app, tap Settings › Ask about EarMind. Something not working? Go to Settings › Feedback and suggestions and tell us what happened.
